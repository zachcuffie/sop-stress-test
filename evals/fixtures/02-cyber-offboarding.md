# Employee Offboarding: Access Removal

Owner: IT Manager
Last reviewed: January 2026

## 1. Purpose
Remove system access for people leaving the company.

## 2. Trigger
The employee's manager emails IT with the employee's last day.

## 3. Steps
1. On the employee's last day, the IT Technician disables the employee's account in the directory.
2. Remove the employee from all systems.
3. Collect the laptop and badge.
4. Forward the employee's email to their manager for 30 days.
5. Close the ticket.

## 4. Notes
Contractors are handled by their agency.
Shared team accounts stay as they are so the team can keep working.
