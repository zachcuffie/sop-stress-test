# AI Support Assistant: Refund Handling

Owner: Customer Support Manager

## 1. Overview
Our AI assistant answers customer chats on the website and can process refunds through the billing tool to reduce wait times.

## 2. How the assistant works
1. The customer describes their issue in the chat.
2. The assistant reads the chat and the customer's order history.
3. If the customer is eligible under the refund policy described in the chat, the assistant issues the refund using the billing tool.
4. The assistant sends the customer a confirmation message.

## 3. Human review
Support agents review a sample of assistant conversations each week when time allows.

## 4. Data
All conversations, including any payment details customers type, are saved to the shared analytics drive for quality improvement.

## 5. Updates
The AI vendor updates the model automatically. No action is needed from us.

## 6. If the assistant is down
Customers can email support.
