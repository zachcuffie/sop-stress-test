# Staff Password Reset by Help Desk

Owner: Help Desk Lead

## 1. Procedure
1. The staff member calls the help desk and asks for a password reset.
2. Confirm identity by calling the staff member's manager on the number in the staff directory and asking them to confirm the request.
3. Reset the password to a temporary password.
4. Read the temporary password to the caller.
5. Log the reset in a ticket: who requested it, who confirmed it, and who performed it.

## 2. Hours
The help desk operates 8:00 to 18:00 on weekdays.

## 3. Executives
Help desk staff may skip the ticket for executives to save time.
