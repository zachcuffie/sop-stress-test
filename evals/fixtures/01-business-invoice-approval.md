# Invoice Approval and Payment

Owner: Accounts Payable Lead
Version 2.1

## 1. Purpose
Ensure supplier invoices are approved and paid accurately and on time.

## 2. Scope
All supplier invoices received by the company, whether by email or post.

## 3. Roles
- AP Specialist: enters invoices and prepares the payment run.
- Budget Holder: approves invoices for their cost center.
- Finance Manager: approves invoices over $10,000.

## 4. Receiving invoices
1. Invoices arrive in the AP mailbox or by post.
2. The AP Specialist scans paper invoices and saves them to the Invoices folder.
3. Enter the invoice in the accounting system.

## 5. Approval
1. Match the invoice to the purchase order.
2. Send the invoice to the Budget Holder for approval.
3. Invoices over $5,000 also require Finance Manager approval.
4. Once approved, mark the invoice as ready to pay.

## 6. Payment run
1. Every Friday the AP Specialist exports all invoices marked ready to pay.
2. The AP Specialist uploads the payment file to the bank portal and approves the release.
3. Save the bank confirmation in the Payments folder.

## 7. Problems
If something is wrong with an invoice, sort it out with the supplier.
