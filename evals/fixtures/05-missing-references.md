# Customer Data Deletion Requests

Owner: Privacy Coordinator

## 1. Purpose
Handle customer requests to delete their personal data.

## 2. Procedure
1. The Support team logs each deletion request as a ticket and assigns it to the Privacy Coordinator.
2. The Privacy Coordinator confirms the requester's identity by sending a verification link to the email address on the customer's account.
3. Check whether any data must be kept under the Records Retention Policy (RP-2, Legal section of the policy library).
4. Complete the usual checklist.
5. Delete the customer's data from the CRM and the billing system.
6. Reply to the customer within the agreed timeline.
7. Record the completion date on the ticket and close it.

## 3. Exceptions
If data must be kept under RP-2, delete everything else and tell the customer what was kept and why.
