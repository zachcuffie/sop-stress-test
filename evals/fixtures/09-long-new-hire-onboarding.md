# New Hire Onboarding

Owner: People Operations Manager
Version 4, March 2026. Reviewed annually by the People Operations Manager.

## 1. Purpose
Make sure every new employee has a signed contract, payroll set up, the equipment and system access they need, and a structured first week.

## 2. Scope
Applies to all new employees, full-time and part-time, in all offices. Contractors follow the Contractor Engagement SOP held in the People folder of the shared drive.

## 3. Roles
- People Operations Manager: owns this SOP and the onboarding checklist.
- People Coordinator: runs the onboarding checklist for each hire.
- Hiring Manager: defines the role's equipment and access needs and runs the first week.
- IT Administrator: prepares equipment and creates accounts.
- Payroll Administrator: sets up the employee in payroll.
- Office Coordinator: arranges desk, badge and building access.

## 4. Trigger
1. Onboarding starts when the candidate returns a signed offer letter to the People Coordinator.
2. The People Coordinator creates an onboarding ticket in the People queue on the same working day, with the start date, role, office and Hiring Manager.

## 5. Pre-start: contract and payroll
1. The People Coordinator sends the employment contract through the e-signature tool within two working days of the trigger.
2. If the contract is not signed five working days before the start date, the People Coordinator tells the Hiring Manager and the People Operations Manager, who decide whether to move the start date.
3. Once the contract is signed, the People Coordinator sends the new hire the payroll form through the e-signature tool.
4. The Payroll Administrator enters the employee in payroll after the form is returned and records the entry date on the ticket.
5. If the payroll form is not returned before the payroll cut-off for the first pay period, the Payroll Administrator tells the People Coordinator, who contacts the new hire the same day.

## 6. Pre-start: equipment and access request
1. The Hiring Manager lists the equipment and system access the role needs.
2. IT prepares the laptop.
3. The IT Administrator creates the directory account and the email account.
4. The IT Administrator grants access to the systems listed by the Hiring Manager and records each grant on the ticket.
5. Accounts are created disabled and are enabled by the IT Administrator on the morning of the start date.

## 7. Pre-start: workspace
1. The Office Coordinator assigns a desk and orders a building badge at least five working days before the start date.
2. For remote employees, the Office Coordinator ships the laptop to the address on the payroll form, with tracked delivery, and records the tracking number on the ticket.
3. If the badge is not ready on the start date, the Office Coordinator issues a temporary visitor badge for up to five working days and records it on the ticket.

## 8. Welcome communication
1. Five working days before the start date, the People Coordinator emails the new hire a welcome note with the start time, office address, first-day contact and what to bring.
2. The Hiring Manager emails the team the day before the start date to introduce the new hire.

## 9. First day
1. The People Coordinator meets the new hire at reception, or on a video call for remote employees, at the start time.
2. The IT Administrator gives the new hire their laptop and helps them sign in and set up multi-factor authentication.
3. The People Coordinator walks through the employee handbook and collects the signed handbook acknowledgment.
4. The Hiring Manager meets the new hire before midday and reviews the first-week plan.

## 10. First week
1. The Hiring Manager runs the first-week plan, including one meeting with each team member the new hire will work with.
2. The new hire completes the mandatory security awareness training in the learning system by the end of the first week.
3. On the last working day of the first week, the Hiring Manager and the new hire hold a check-in and the Hiring Manager records any open needs on the ticket.

## 11. Access adjustments
1. If the new hire needs access not on the original list, they ask their Hiring Manager.
2. The new hire can also ask a colleague who already has the access to share their login until IT grants it.
3. The IT Administrator grants additional access when the Hiring Manager requests it on the ticket.

## 12. If the start is cancelled or delayed
1. If the offer is withdrawn or the candidate does not start, the People Coordinator updates the ticket and tells the Hiring Manager, IT Administrator, Payroll Administrator and Office Coordinator.
2. The Payroll Administrator removes the payroll entry.
3. The Office Coordinator cancels the badge.

## 13. Records
1. The ticket is closed by the People Coordinator after the first-week check-in once every checklist item is complete.
2. The signed contract, payroll form and handbook acknowledgment are stored in the employee file.
3. Onboarding tickets are kept according to company policy.

## 14. Exceptions
1. If the start date is less than five working days after the trigger, the People Coordinator runs Sections 5 to 8 on a compressed timeline and records which deadlines were missed on the ticket.
2. Accounts may be enabled up to two days before the start date if the Hiring Manager requests early access for pre-reading.
